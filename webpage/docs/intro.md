# Introduction

Problem Statement
I am currently in my second year as an intern at ATOS, a consultancy firm that serves both public and private sector clients. At the end of my first year, I participated in a hackathon focused on improving internal processes through the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Over the course of three months, my team and I developed a project aimed at supporting the sales and pre-sales departments in analyzing public bidding documents (RFPs), with the goal of identifying business opportunities where the company could participate.

One of the major challenges ATOS faces is interpreting bidding documents, which can be up to 800 pages long or even more. This process is extremely time-consuming and often does not yield concrete results, as not all analyzed documents translate into contracts for the company. The slow pace of this analysis has a significant impact, leading to lost opportunities that could generate millions in revenue, strengthen client relationships, and open doors to new areas of work.

Therefore, the core problem lies in the need to accelerate and optimize the interpretation of bidding documents, reducing the time spent on this phase and increasing the efficiency in identifying real opportunities for the company.

Project Overview
The main objective of this project is to develop an integrated system based on Large Language Models (LLMs), enabling users to upload bidding documents and automatically receive an output containing the technical and financial requirements of the project. Additionally, the tool performs a cross-check between these requirements and the company’s existing certifications and attestations, ensuring compliance with the prerequisites for participating in the bidding process.

This solution was designed to assist the sales and pre-sales teams by providing a more efficient and streamlined analysis of opportunities, optimizing work time, and increasing agility throughout the bidding process. As a result, the company can enhance its presence in the public sector and improve its competitiveness in the market.
