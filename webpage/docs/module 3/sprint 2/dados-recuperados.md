## 📑 4 - Key Information from Public Notice SP001/2024 – EMBASA
---
An entire public notice document was used to test and extract all the necessary requirements for a company to qualify for this specific bidding process. The notice used was from 'Embasa – Bahia State Government'.

### 1. **Economic and Financial Requirements**
- The company must present the **balance sheet from the last fiscal year**, prepared in accordance with the law, and **it cannot be replaced by trial balances or provisional statements** [1].
- A **minimum net worth** must be demonstrated, as specified in the preamble of the notice. If the bidder participates in more than one lot, the required amount is the sum of each lot’s value [1].
- Certain **minimum financial ratios** must be proven (not specified in the excerpt, but typically include General Liquidity, Solvency, etc.) [1].
- The company must also prove **net financial availability (DFL)**. If bidding on multiple lots, the total required DFL is the sum needed for each lot [1].
- In the case of **consortia**, the required DFL is calculated in proportion to each consortium member's participation share [1].
- Companies undergoing **judicial recovery** may participate, as long as they prove economic and financial capability as defined in the notice [1].
- **Micro and small businesses** must meet the same economic and financial requirements [1].

---
### 2. **Alternative Accounting Documentation**
- If the notice has been **published in the Official Gazette**, that publication may be used as proof instead of submitting the actual balance sheet [1].
- If the company’s **fiscal year differs from the calendar year**, it must submit documentation to prove this exception [1].

---
### 3. **Bidding Procedures**
- The public notice follows as its legal basis **Law No. 13.303/2016**, applicable to publicly owned companies, as well as **Complementary Law No. 123/2006**, which addresses special provisions for micro and small enterprises [1].
- The bidding will be judged according to this **public notice**, its **annexes**, and the **Internal Regulation for Bids and Contracts of EMBASA** [1].
